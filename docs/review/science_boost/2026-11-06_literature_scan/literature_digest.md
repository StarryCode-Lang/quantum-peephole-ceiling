## 3. Precision read: arXiv:2608.27592 (Plateau-Constrained Selection)

Full abstract + HTML v2 full text read this session. Methodology essentials:

1. **Setting**: two-stage compilation of commuting phase terms with fixed
   placement and maintained-parity lowering. Stage 1 attains the support
   optimum (OR-Tools, 5 s budget; `2n+2` certifies optimality via Hamiltonian
   paths of the support line graph); Stage 2 selects minimum routed depth
   among **K=24 equal-cost orders** generated by zero-cost local moves.
2. **Evidence standard**: paired bootstrap CIs (10,000 resamples), exact
   Wilcoxon signed-rank, Holm families + a 64-test global sensitivity analysis;
   Spearman rank correlation 0.910 across SABRE seeds (selection transfers).
3. **Numbers**: equal-support-cost-74 orders route to mean depths
   228.6 / 233.8 / 256.7; 12.83% depth gain vs a specific Stage-1 baseline,
   **14.77% and 22.39% against the other two Stage-1 formulations** — the
   reported magnitude is baseline-relative; gains reverse under BasicSwap
   (rank correlation 0.093); Heron error-reduction CI includes 0.
4. **Limitations (their own)**: no guarantee of global minimum routed depth;
   counts exact only to 20 terms; samples 24 orders, not the full plateau;
   saturation not established at 24; protocol stops rather than silently
   relaxing the optimum.
5. **Overlap axes**: none of the four (no reachability criterion, no
   decidability, no counting law, no completeness price).

**Demarcation sentence (for the paper)**: they optimize the *selection inside
the plateau*; we decide *whether the plateau is the only route*.

**Adopted for P0-1**: their statistics bar (paired bootstrap CI + exact
Wilcoxon + Holm families) becomes the reporting standard for cross-source
tables; their three-baseline spread is direct external evidence for the P1-1
benchmark-accounting thesis.
